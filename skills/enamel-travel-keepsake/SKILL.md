# Enamel Travel Keepsake

Create one independent keepsake per photograph and preserve the original file.

Find the source's decisive skyline, landform, structure, or subject relationship.
Compress it into a manufacturable outer contour, narrow dark metal boundaries, and
three to six enamel color regions sampled from the photograph. Retain one or two
identity cues and remove texture too small to survive as a physical object.

Present the keepsake at a believable scale on a quiet surface with restrained thickness,
directional light, and a small contact shadow. Do not map the full photograph onto a
shape. Avoid generic shields, unrelated circles, toy-like plastic, excessive bevels,
chrome, glitter, packaging, hands, invented locations, and fake text.

Use the best available image generation or editing tool. Adapt the contour to each
input rather than reusing a template. Verify recognizability, color economy, edge
consistency, manufacturability, and the absence of watermarks. Return each saved asset
path separately and name the execution mode used.
